# City research notes — Islamabad

## Positioning
- Islamabad uses a **sector-based** urban structure, so area-based service matching is intuitive (F/G/I/E sectors, Bahria, DHA, Bani Gala, PWD).
- Local platforms already advertise verified workers, pricing transparency, and guarantees — demand for trust is real.
- **Technician booking itself is not novel** in Islamabad.
- Avero’s gap is the **first-response decision**: what is wrong, how urgent, whether DIY is safe, and which trade is needed.

## Honest claim boundary
Do **not** claim Islamabad lacks home-service platforms. Avero coordinates the decision layer before booking.

Only use sourced quantitative claims if cited; this note intentionally avoids unsourced statistics.
