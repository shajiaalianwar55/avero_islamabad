# AI Safety

- Deterministic patterns in `lib/safety/rules.ts` catch gas, smoke, sparks, burning smell, water+electric, etc.
- AI classifier may add caution/emergency signals; merge always prefers `stop_troubleshooting`.
- DIY is blocked when emergency is active.
- Structured AI outputs are Zod-validated with one retry + safe fallback.
- Hidden chain-of-thought is never shown to residents — only facts, concerns, decision, next step.

Avero is **not** a substitute for emergency services.
